Yaadly

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-28

Yaadly is a WhatsApp assistant that turns your messages and voice notes into calendar events and list items. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp number and profile name, to know who a message is from and reply to you.
  • Messages, voice notes and photos you send to Yaadly. Voice notes are transcribed; the audio itself is processed in memory and not stored. We keep the text and transcript to show your history and improve accuracy.
  • Events and lists you create, and your settings (language, time zone).
  • Google Calendar access, only if you connect it: an access token (stored encrypted) that lets Yaadly read your calendars' busy times and events and add or change the events Yaadly creates.

How we use it

Only to provide the service: understanding your request, saving events and lists, checking for clashes, and replying on WhatsApp. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for marketing.

Who processes it

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) delivers messages between you and Yaadly.
  • Groq runs the speech-to-text and language models that understand your messages.
  • Google, if you connect Google Calendar.

Storage and security

Data is stored on our server in a PostgreSQL database. Connections use HTTPS, Google tokens are encrypted at rest, and access is limited to the operator of the service.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while you use Yaadly. You can disconnect Google at any time from your Google Account. To delete all your data, see Data deletion.
